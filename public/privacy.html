Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-28

1. Data we collect

Compressly is a static client-side application. Your images never leave your device. All compression happens in your browser via WebAssembly. We do not run a file upload server.

2. Analytics

Compressly uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It is cookieless, collects no personal data, and does not build an advertising profile from your visit. We run no other third-party analytics or tracking scripts, and we never see the images you process.

3. Payments & licenses

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, Inc., our Merchant of Record. They collect billing details directly — we never see your card. License keys you enter into Compressly are validated against the Lemon Squeezy API and cached in localStorage on your device.
